## 1. Executive Summary

At the request of leadership, the EduPulse AI web application has undergone a complete, non-destructive UI/UX overhaul. The application has transitioned from an experimental, dense, dark-mode prototype into an authoritative, clean, minimalist **white enterprise decision-support platform** inspired by Tier-1 strategy consulting analytical briefings (McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte).

### Key Accomplishments
1. **100% Frontend-Only Execution:** Not a single backend query, SQL view, DuckDB table, analytical formula, or Pydantic API contract was modified. All data integrity, risk formulas, and warehouse connections remain intact.
2. **Consulting Presentation Standard:** Pure white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) on a subtle slate canvas (`#F8FAFC`), crisp hair-line borders (`#E2E8F0`), high-contrast slate typography (`#0F172A`), and disciplined semantic accents (`#059669`, `#D97706`, `#DC2626`, `#0284C7`).
3. **Comprehensive Screen Redesign:** All 8 primary analytical views, interactive data tables, Recharts visualizations, the School 360 diagnostic profile, the AI Decision Analyst workbench, and the floating AI Copilot modal were redesigned.
4. **Flawless Automated Quality Assurance:**
   - **Vitest Unit Tests:** 9 / 9 passed (100%)
   - **Playwright E2E Tests:** 4 / 4 passed (100%)
   - **Next.js Production Build:** 10 / 10 routes compiled with 0 errors

## 2. Redesigned Pages & Components Overview

|---|---|---|
| **Global App Shell** | Layout, Sidebar, Header, Global Filters | Clean 230px white sidebar, subtle sky active states, crisp header with Data Trust pill, white filter dropdowns |
| **Executive Overview (`/`)** | High-level state education command center | 4 white KPI cards with benchmark delta pills, 5-stage Data Trust lineage cards, 3 editorial insight alert banners, district ranking chart |

| **School 360 Profile (`/schools/[id]`)** | Deep-dive institutional root-cause diagnosis | Clean institutional header, 5-point physical amenities checklist, historical attendance/FLN line chart, operational policy action memo |

| **Procurement & Nutrition (`/procurement`)** | Mid-Day Meal public spend governance | Spend KPI cards with currency formatting, district expenditure bar chart, peer benchmark outlier table |
| **Intervention Command (`/intervention`)** | Risk triaging and operational resource allocation | Severity triaging cards, composite risk vs priority score quadrant matrix, high-priority school action queue |
| **Data Trust & Quality (`/quality`)** | 10 automated quality gates and lineage | 6 governance KPI cards, 5-stage pipeline flow, 10 automated quality gates audit table (100% pass), traceable KPI lineage contracts |

| **Global AI Copilot (`components/chatbot`)** | Floating contextual assistant | Clean white modal popup, quick question carousel, real-time reasoning progress bar, zero-hallucination DuckDB verification |
